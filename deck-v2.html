Market Gap

A $135B Industry Runs on Gut Feel

There is no centralized system to quantify an athlete's commercial value. This is not a gap — it is a market.

5 questions no CMO can answer

  • Brand fit — does this athlete match our audience?
  • Longevity — will they still be relevant in 3 years?
  • Audience quality — real reach or vanity followers?
  • Market rate — what is a fair price for this deal?
  • ROI — what return did the last campaign actually drive?
  • Decisions get made on gut feel — with no predictability.

Who pays first

  • Brand CMOs and sponsorship agencies buying certainty.
  • Federations mapping and monetizing their talent pipeline.
  • The commercial buyer — not the fan — is the wedge.

Brands, athletes, and federations all demonstrably lose money to fragmented silos — Catapult, OpenSponsorship, and agency spreadsheets don't talk to each other.

Business Model

Core Monetisation at Seed. Infrastructure at Scale.

We charge for access to intelligence that reduces commercial risk. At the Seed stage, we are ruthlessly focused on two revenue streams.

1 · Brand Intelligence SaaS
(yearly subscription)

  • Who pays: Brand CMOs & sponsorship agencies
  • Product: athlete brand-fit scoring & predictive ROI modelling
  • Unit economics: ₹15K–25K / year per brand seat · 80%+ gross margin

2 · Deal Intelligence Commission

  • Who pays: brands executing deals via the ADC engine
  • Product: data-backed athlete endorsement contracting
  • Unit economics: 10–20% take rate (₹50K–10L per deal)

Phase 3 infrastructure play (Series A+): once the dataset achieves liquidity (50,000+ athletes), ADC becomes an API layer — per-query access for third-party platforms, fantasy leagues, and scouting desks building on our data spine.
